About the platform

Bridging modeling and real-world decisions

An open-access decision-support tool from the Power Transformation Lab at UC San Diego, translating sector-wide transition studies into facility-specific, investment-ready guidance for park owners, PLN, provincial governments, and financiers.

Data & methodology

The platform is built on a generation & transmission capacity-expansion model (Julia/JuMP) that co-optimizes industrial-facility and grid investments, drawing on PLN, MEMR, JETP and the Sunrise Project captive-power database.
