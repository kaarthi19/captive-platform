About

The Power Transformation Lab

The Power Transformation Lab at UC San Diego studies the engineering and institutional requirements of deploying low-carbon energy at scale. Working across many geographies with academic, government, civil-society and industry partners, the lab builds research, open models and data for climate and environmental challenges.

This platform is an open-access decision-support tool from the lab, translating sector-wide transition studies into facility-specific, investment-ready guidance for park owners, PLN, provincial governments, and financiers.

Data & methodology

Results come from GARUDA (Grid And Renewable Utilization & Deployment Analysis), the lab's generation and transmission capacity-expansion model, which co-optimizes industrial-facility and grid investments using PLN, MEMR and JETP planning data. Site, ownership and financing data come from the Earthwise Institute Indonesian Power Summary Database.
